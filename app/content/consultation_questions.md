# Sample question sets

Stand-in for the public repository. The practice's real question sets encode its own method and stay private; this file keeps the same format.
After the client's first answer, the consultation asks the set that matches their main issue, in this order, one question at a time. The notes in brackets say why a question is asked: they are for the consultant only and are never read out.

## Smoking
1. Have you tried to quit before? If so, what methods did you use, and what were the results?
2. When do you usually feel the strongest urge to smoke? [triggers]
3. What would being a non-smoker change in your daily life? [motivation]

## Drinking alcohol
1. How would you describe your drinking now, and how has it changed over time?
2. What tends to happen just before you have a drink? [triggers]
3. What would you like your relationship with alcohol to look like? [goal]

## Anxiety
1. When did you first notice the anxiety, and what was happening in your life then?
2. Where do you feel it in your body? [body signals]
3. What do you do to cope at the moment, and how well does it work?

## Depression
1. How has your mood been over the past few weeks?
2. What parts of your day feel hardest, and which feel easier? [pattern]
3. Are you being supported by a doctor or therapist at the moment? [safety and referral]

## Insomnia
1. What does a typical night look like for you?
2. What goes through your mind when you cannot sleep? [thought pattern]
3. What have you already tried to help you sleep?

## Lack of confidence
1. In which situations does your confidence drop most?
2. What do you say to yourself in those moments? [inner voice]
3. What would you do differently if you felt confident?

## Panic attacks
1. When did you last have a panic attack, and what happened?
2. What are the first signs you notice? [early signals]
3. What do you fear might happen during an attack?

## Past experience
1. Is there a past experience that you would like to feel differently about?
2. How does it affect you today? [impact]
3. Is there support in place for you around this? [safety and referral]

## PTSD
1. Please only share what you are comfortable sharing. What would you most like support with? [safety: do not probe details]
2. Are you working with a doctor or trauma therapist? [referral]
3. What helps you to feel safe and grounded? [resources]

## Self-worth
1. How would you describe the way you see yourself?
2. Where do you think that view came from? [origins]
3. What would change if you valued yourself more?
